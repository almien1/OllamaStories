Quant cheat sheet

Bigger K number and no "I" prefix generally means closer to full quality and more disk/VRAM. For roleplay, dialogue nuance is more quant-sensitive than trivia recall, so it's worth spending VRAM on Q5_K_M over Q4_K_M when a model has room to spare.

QuantBits/weightTypical use
Q4_K_M~4.8Safe default; noticeable but minor quality loss
Q5_K_M~5.7Sweet spot when VRAM allows — prefer this over Q4_K_M
Q6_K~6.6Close to full quality, meaningfully larger
Q8_0~8.5Effectively lossless; rarely worth the VRAM on an 8–13B model
IQ3_M / IQ4_XS~3–4Importance-weighted quants for squeezing large (20B+) models into limited VRAM

min_p 0.05 with a fairly open top_p/top_k keeps unlikely-but-fitting words on the table — important for voice and personality — while still cutting off the truly incoherent tail. --jinja applies the chat template baked into each GGUF, which matters more than usual here since several roleplay finetunes (Metharme, ChatML variants) expect a specific turn format to stay in character.
